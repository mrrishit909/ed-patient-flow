ED Patient Flow

An invented emergency department at 07:00 on a Tuesday. Arrivals, admissions and beds forecast from its own history; the department run forward many times from its live state; a surge and a ward slowdown; actions simulated before a person approves them. …
The department, its patients, staff and beds are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real patient data is used and nothing here is clinical advice. Encounters carry a hashed token and an age band only. No outside model or service is called.
